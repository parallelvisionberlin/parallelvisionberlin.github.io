Berlin 2063

An evolving audiovisual archive of a possible future.

Visual Archive / Berlin 2063

Berlin 2063 is an evolving audiovisual archive exploring how a city and the people within it might change across the coming decades.

Through architecture, infrastructure, technology, nightlife, fashion and everyday life, the archive examines how systems already present today could reshape the way we move, gather, work, dress and relate to one another.

The archive does not end on screen. Its images, characters and environments can be translated into projection, performance and physical space.

Nina is one of its inhabitants—and the only one visitors can speak to directly.
