01 / VISUAL ARCHIVE

Hyperfuture Berlin

Hyperfuture Berlin is a speculative extension of the Berlin 2063 archive, looking further into the city’s possible futures. Here synthetic inhabitants, monumental civic interiors, new forms of mobility and altered social relations have become ordinary parts of urban life. The archive focuses on how that future is lived: how people gather, move, form attachments and share space with non-human beings.
