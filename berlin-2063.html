Visual Archive / Berlin 2063

Berlin 2063 begins with a familiar city. These selected images follow its spaces, its inhabitants, its life after dark and its robots.

The films travel further into the hyperfuture. The still images return to the scale of a street, a carriage or an encounter. Together, they form an imagined Berlin across different possible futures.
