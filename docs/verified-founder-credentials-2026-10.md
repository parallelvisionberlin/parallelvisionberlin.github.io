# Alejandro Molinari / Parallel Vision
## Verified career credentials for grants and partnerships
Updated: 26 October 2026. Editorial source register, not an award declaration or a list of Parallel Vision clients.

### Positioning
Alejandro Molinari is a Venezuelan-born producer, musician and performer based in Berlin. His independent recording and performance history precedes the founding of Parallel Vision in 2025. Parallel Vision now brings together original music, moving image, the Berlin 2063 world, and the Fashion After Fabric research project. The evidence below demonstrates a continuity of production, fashion-film contributions, public performances and independent editorial recognition.

### Short bio for grant and partnership documents
Alejandro Molinari is a Venezuelan-born, Berlin-based musician, producer and creative director. His recording career has included releases through Nein Records, Italians Do It Better and ORIANNA / Sony Music España, editorial music contributions to fashion films, and performances across Europe including Fusion Festival. His work has been documented by Bandcamp Daily, Purple Magazine, KALTBLUT and other independent outlets. He founded Parallel Vision in Berlin in 2025, expanding a long-running musical practice into a label and multidisciplinary studio linking original music, imagined urban futures and moving image. Through Berlin 2063 and Fashion After Fabric, the studio develops recurring visual environments, characters and material concepts across films, images and music.
