Profile / Melodic Transmission

Ëlorian is an Italian-born, Mexico-based producer and DJ whose music moves between melodic tension, progressive electronics and the physical pull of the club.

Working across Mexico and Europe, his trajectory connects different rooms and different temperatures: Mexico City after-hours, Berlin rooftops, European clubs and the slower emotional architecture of melodic electronic music. Rather than treating melody as decoration, Ëlorian builds around it, letting repetition, pressure and release gradually reshape the track.

His releases have appeared across Enormous Vision, Asymmetric Recordings, Freegrant Music, LW Recordings and Parallel Vision. Inside the Parallel Vision archive, his remix of Alejandro Molinari’s Tanzen Im Kreis pushes the original into a more expansive state: hypnotic, emotional and designed to keep unfolding after the first impact.
