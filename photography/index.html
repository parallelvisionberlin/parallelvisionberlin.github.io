Alejandro Molinari.

Berlin-based photographer, artist and music producer. Working with Fujifilm and Canon, his photographic practice explores night flash, nocturnal figures, forest encounters and intimate interiors.

Son of Oscar Molinari Herrera (1941–2017), the Italian-Venezuelan experimental filmmaker and photographer who visited Salvador Dalí in Cadaqués in 1972 and helped establish the Taller de Cine Independiente in Caracas in 1973.
