Alejandro Molinari.

PHOTOGRAPHIC PRACTICE

Berlin-based photographer and music producer. His work explores night flash, forest studies, the human figure and intimate interiors, photographed with Fujifilm and Canon cameras.

ARTISTIC LINEAGE

Son of Oscar Molinari Herrera (1941–2017), experimental filmmaker and photographer. Oscar visited Salvador Dalí in Cadaqués in 1972 and helped establish the Taller de Cine Independiente in Caracas in 1973.
