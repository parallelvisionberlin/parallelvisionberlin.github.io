001–007

PHOTOGRAPHIC WORK / FUJIFILM & CANON

Night flash, forest studies and intimate interiors.

Berlin-based photographer and music producer. The photographs were made with Fujifilm and Canon cameras.

ARTISTIC LINEAGE

His father, Oscar Molinari Herrera (1941–2017), was an experimental filmmaker and photographer. He visited Salvador Dalí in Cadaqués in 1972 and helped establish the Taller de Cine Independiente in Caracas in 1973.
