Alejandro Molinari.

FUJIFILM / CANON · SELECTED PHOTOGRAPHS

01 / PRACTICE

Berlin-based photographer, artist and music producer. His photographs move between night flash, forest studies, the human figure and intimate interiors.

02 / LINEAGE

Son of Oscar Molinari Herrera (1941–2017), an experimental filmmaker and photographer who visited Salvador Dalí in Cadaqués in 1972 and helped establish the Taller de Cine Independiente in Caracas in 1973.
