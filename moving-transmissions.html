The visual archive

Berlin 2063 is the point of departure: urban form, transit, nightlife and the people within it. Moving Transmissions brings this archive into motion and follows it further into the hyperfuture.

The selected still images move through the city, its inhabitants and its life after dark. The films extend these encounters into more distant futures.

Projection / Collaboration

From screen
to space

Moving Transmissions is a growing collection of Parallel Vision films for projection, exhibitions and live performances. Selections can be developed around a room, its screen proportions and the music.

For a space such as Studio 11.11, this could take the form of projected films responding to each performance, loops between sets, or an extended journey through the archive.
