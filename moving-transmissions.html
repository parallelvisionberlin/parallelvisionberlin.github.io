01 / Motion Layer

Moving Transmissions are the motion layer of Berlin 2063: short visual loops built for screens, rooms and live environments.

They are not trailers. They are atmosphere systems: fragments of transit, nightlife, bodies, machines and future materials designed to live between performances.
