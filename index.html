Parallel Vision / Living Archive

Berlin 2063

Enter the world

Berlin 2063 is an evolving audiovisual archive built from imagined environments, inhabitants and memories of a possible future.

Through film, sound, fashion, architecture and artificial intelligence, the project constructs a city shaped by the systems already transforming contemporary life.

It is not presented as a distant spectacle, but as a lived world with routines, contradictions, desires and personal histories.

Parallel Vision / Material Research

Fashion After Fabric

The question is no longer what we will wear.
It is what clothing becomes once fabric disappears.

Fashion After Fabric is a speculative material research project exploring what clothing becomes when textiles are replaced by responsive, biological and synthetic systems.

Each collection develops a distinct material hypothesis—from liquid metal and programmable surfaces to engineered tissue, magnetic structures and living membranes.

Material Research for Bodies in Transition

Fashion After Fabric explores what clothing becomes when textiles are replaced by responsive, biological and synthetic systems.

Each collection develops a distinct material hypothesis—from liquid metal and programmable surfaces to engineered tissue, magnetic structures and living membranes.

Together, they form an evolving archive of possible relationships between clothing, technology and the human body.

Collection 01 / Reactive Alloy

Chromia

CHROMIA imagines liquid metal future clothing as a second skin: monochrome alloy, mercury movement, chrome membranes and garments that react to heat, sound and pulse.

Liquid metal Reactive alloy Body-interface Berlin 2063 catwalk
Collection 02 / Liquid Metal Collection

Flesh Zero

Flesh Zero is the point where the body stops being dressed and begins being replaced. Chrome, gas, foam and volatile synthetic tissue form a ceremonial surface around the human silhouette. The collection belongs to the Parallel Vision Berlin 2063 timeline and expands the Liquid Metal language into something more organic, ritualistic and unstable than Chromia.

Liquid chrome Gas / Foam / Smoke Ritual body mutation Berlin 2063
Collection 03 / Tropical-Dark Ceremony

Lotus 2063

LOTUS 2063 is black high fashion inflated into oversized ritual silhouettes: elegant, brutal, ceremonial and built for humid club corridors after midnight.

Oversized black Tropical-dark Ceremonial Future nightlife
Collection 04 / Analog Signal Garments

Magnetic Tape

Magnetic Tape turns archive media into body architecture: signal ribbons, black reflective strips, recorded movement and clothing that feels pulled from a damaged future cassette.

Magnetic tape Analog archive Signal strips Body recording
Collection 05 / Living Garments

DNA Mutation

The upcoming bio-material collection treats fashion as mutation: bacterial surfaces, spikes, genetic alteration, living membranes and garments that behave like organisms.

Bacteria Spikes Genetic modification Body mutation
