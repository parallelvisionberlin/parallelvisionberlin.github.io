About this world

A Bright Future explores a possible society in which technological development supports social progress. Humans, robots and AI share public space, care and forms of attachment. The Hyperfuture studies ask what advancement means at the scale of daily life: how a city can make room for coexistence, dignity and mutual dependence.

One of three parallel possibilities in Berlin 2063 and Beyond, explored through images, film, sound and material studies.
