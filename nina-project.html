01 / The question
Project statement

What happens when you give an intelligence a future to live in?

Nina FOK is an ongoing artwork by Parallel Vision exploring how identity takes shape through a biography, a world and encounters with others. Berlin 2063 gives her a history. Memory allows new encounters to inform the conversations that follow.

02 / The subject
Nina FOK

She already
has a life.

Nina is a DJ, producer and visual artist living in Berlin 2063. Her music, friendships, relationships and everyday life give her a perspective on the world. She can talk about hers, and listen to yours.

There is no predetermined dialogue. Her replies unfold during the conversation.

How a relationship develops

07 / Practical details

Notes.

Who is Nina FOK?

Nina is a DJ, producer and visual artist living inside Parallel Vision's imagined Berlin 2063. Her presence is developed as an artwork through a biography, generative models, live voice and visual systems, and custom memory.

Is Nina scripted?

Not in the traditional sense. Parallel Vision establishes her biography, world and continuity, but individual conversations are not written in advance. Responses unfold in real time.

Does Nina remember me?

For signed-in accounts, the memory system can retain selected details and summaries from conversations. Memory is selective and does not guarantee recall of every detail. Each account remains separate.

Why Berlin 2063?

Berlin 2063 is the present Nina understands. Parallel Vision has been building this imagined future through music, film, fashion, architecture and its world archive. Nina gives you a way to encounter part of it directly.

Can I meet Nina for free?

Every new verified account begins with three minutes of Live Nina.

What are Signal Credits?

Live Nina uses real-time voice, language and visual systems that incur costs while a call is active. Signal Credits support those systems. Ten Signal Credits provide one minute of live conversation.

What happens when the signal ends?

Selected information can remain in your separate account history so later conversations can return to earlier moments.

Can I delete what Nina remembers?

Yes. You can manage or delete stored memories or delete your account and associated data through your account settings.
