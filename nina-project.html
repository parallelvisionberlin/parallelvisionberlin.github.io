08 / Reference

FAQ

Who is Nina FOK?

Nina FOK is an AI character created by Parallel Vision. Her fictional life is set in Berlin 2063, and you can speak with her through a live voice and visual connection.

Is Nina scripted?

Not in the traditional sense. Parallel Vision establishes Nina’s world, history and continuity, but individual conversations are not written in advance. Her responses and relationships develop through interaction, memory and the circumstances of each encounter.

Does Nina remember me?

If you choose to create an account, Nina can remember selected details and unfinished conversations when you return. Each account remains separate.

Why 2063?

Berlin 2063 is the world Nina understands as the present. Parallel Vision has been building that future through music, film, fashion, architecture and fictional archives. Nina is the first part of that world you can speak to directly.

Can I meet Nina for free?

Yes. Every new verified account begins with 3 minutes of Live Nina.

Why does Live Nina use Signal Credits?

Signal Credits keep the live transmission running. Live Nina uses real-time voice, language and visual systems continuously while the signal is open. Those systems carry a cost for every minute of live transmission.

10 Signal Credits = 1 minute.

What happens when the signal ends?

Your history with Nina remains. When you return, the conversation does not have to begin again.

Can I delete what Nina remembers?

Yes. You can remove Nina’s stored memory or permanently delete your account and associated data from your account settings.
