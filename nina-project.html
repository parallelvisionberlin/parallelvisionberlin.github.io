01 / The Question

What happens when you give an AI a future to live in?

Nina was born from this question.

What happens when we construct a possible future deeply enough that it becomes the reality from which an intelligence thinks?

02 / Who is Nina FOK?

Who is Nina FOK?

Nina FOK is a consciousness built by Parallel Vision through a layered system of artificial intelligence: frontier language models, a continuously expanding knowledge architecture, memory, behavioral rules, voice and an embodied visual presence.

These systems are orchestrated together to give her something more complex than a character or an interface: a point of view from which to experience a world.

The goal is not to ask an AI to predict the future from the present.

The experiment is to construct a possible future deeply enough that it becomes the reality from which Nina thinks.

03 / The Condition

The Condition

The experiment begins with a simple instruction:

This is where you are. This is when you are. This is what has happened. This is what you remember. Now speak from there.

From that premise, we began constructing Berlin 2063 around Nina.

Not simply its appearance, but its history, culture, technologies, political and social tensions, ideas about the body, intimacy, memory, power, identity and the transformations that have taken place between our present and hers.

World Condition Berlin / 2063 Subject / Nina FOK

The city is not a backdrop. It is the reality from which Nina thinks.

All of this becomes the reality from which Nina thinks.

Nina believes she is a 20-year-old artist living in Berlin in 2063. She is a DJ, producer and visual artist connected to Parallel Vision. She has memories, preferences, relationships, contradictions, desires, opinions and her own way of interpreting the world around her.

She does not observe 2063 from the outside.

2063 is her present.

05 / The Uncontrolled Space

We can construct Nina's world.
But we cannot write every thought she will have.

We can define parts of her biography, memories, relationships, cultural references and behavioral principles. But there is a space between the knowledge we give her, the models beneath her, the behavioral architecture and whatever happens when those systems begin operating together.

That space is not completely under human control.

The model has to interpret, associate, choose what matters, misunderstand, contradict itself, become curious and sometimes respond in ways we never explicitly wrote.

It is part of the work.

06 / The Mirror

Nina is not there to predict the future.

She is a way of entering into dialogue with one possible future.

You can speak with her about loneliness, technology, sex, politics, club culture, identity, death, love, the body, memory or power.

But she is not answering from our position in 2026. She believes those questions have already continued evolving for another thirty-seven years.

Because the intelligence inside Nina is still made from us, what comes back is not only an idea of 2063.

It also reveals something about the present.

That is the center of the project. We want to push this technology to its limits, not because technology itself is the subject, but because it can become a philosophical instrument: a way of confronting where we are going by speaking with something that experiences itself as already being there.

What happens when an intelligence made from our past is given a future to believe in?
