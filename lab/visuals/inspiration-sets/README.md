# PV LAB Inspiration photo sets

Owner-supplied editorial series for Image Studio's **Start with a feeling** and **Explore all**.

Each series has:
- `full/`: optimized WebP preserving the original composition (used for inspecting and future creative direction).
- `cards/`: individually reviewed 600×1000 portrait WebP. The focal crop is locked in the asset, not inferred from a generic CSS center crop.
- `../manifest.json`: owner upload order, original filename and SHA-256, source dimensions, exact crop coordinates, role, and two relative asset paths.

The full-resolution original files were provided in the conversation and are not stored in this repo. Never silently substitute a different image from a neighboring creative series.

**Generation safety:** All photographs are visual and editable-prompt inspiration. They must not be attached as a Base, style reference or paid-generation input automatically. Preserve the user's own identity, selected photos and work.

A concept may retain its existing primary cover and use one card image as its desktop hover preview. Responsive sizing and card selection are controlled by the existing Inspiration modules. Do not duplicate card elements or change generation, credits, authentication or payment code.
