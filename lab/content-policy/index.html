Content & Safety Policy

Updated 17 October 2026

PV LAB is designed for expressive visual work, including fashion, portraiture and lawful adult artistic nudity where the selected models and applicable law permit it. Creative freedom does not include exploitation or abuse.

Not permitted

  • Sexual exploitation of minors: any sexualized depiction of a child or a person presented as a child, including photorealistic, edited or entirely AI-generated content, is prohibited. A written claim that a childlike subject is “18+” does not override the depiction.
  • Non-consensual sexual content: intimate deepfakes of identifiable people without consent, sexual coercion or sexual assault depictions, and exploitation are prohibited.
  • Abuse of real people's images: identity training, impersonation, intimate editing or other uses of real people's likenesses without the necessary authorization are not allowed.
  • Other unlawful content: fraud, threats, unlawful harassment, privacy violations, unlawful discrimination or infringement of another person's rights are prohibited.
  • Safety and account misuse: circumventing safeguards, repeated free-trial farming, and misuse of disposable addresses to obtain promotional credits are prohibited.

Two people kissing, a non-sexual portrayal of younger people, different ethnicities, gender expressions or same-sex relationships are not, on their own, policy violations. Decisions depend on the nature and context of the content, not the identities of the people depicted.

What happens when content is reported

PV LAB may reject a generation, restrict access, suspend an account, or remove prohibited material. A report is reviewed based on available evidence and applicable law. The selected model provider may independently reject requests. No automated filter can guarantee that all inappropriate results are blocked.

Where preservation or notification is legally required, PV LAB may retain the minimum necessary records for that purpose. Otherwise, content is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and Terms. Customers may request human review of restrictions.

Report an issue

If you encounter content that may violate these rules, email contact@parallelvisionlabel.com with a job ID, account ID or brief description. Do not send or forward potentially illegal images. We will explain the next steps as appropriate.
