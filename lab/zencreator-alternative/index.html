04 / Before you begin

A few things worth knowing.

Can I try PV LAB before buying credits?

Yes. Eligible newly verified accounts receive 200 one-time welcome credits without a purchase. Explore the tools and Moods, then check the quote before generating. Some jobs cost more than the welcome balance.

Can I use an image as the start of a video?

Yes. Create an image, send it to Video Studio and animate it with a supported model. You can also use existing uploads and supported references.

What usage rules apply?

PV LAB is for people aged 18 and over. Only upload material you have the rights and permissions to use, including consent for identity training. Your use of every model must comply with PV LAB's Terms, the applicable provider's rules and the law. Requests may be refused or restricted.

Is PV LAB cheaper than ZenCreator?

That depends on model, duration, resolution, audio and purchased credit pack. The platforms' credits are not interchangeable. Compare the exact quote before generating on either platform.
