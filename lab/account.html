Delete your PV Lab account?

Closing PV Lab immediately disables new generations and purchases, then queues permanent deletion of your images, videos, prompts, moods and references. Cleanup may take time and cannot be undone. Download anything you want to keep first. Cancel any subscription and let running jobs finish before continuing.

Your separate Parallel Vision Label sign-in and Nina memory are not deleted. Required billing and limited abuse-prevention records are retained. You will be signed out when closure is accepted; the dedicated PV LAB Clerk identity is removed after creative-data cleanup is verified.
