## Proposed sales terms

1. **Service and account.** PV Lab is an online creative service for generating and editing images and video. The contracting business is **Alejandro Molinari, trading as PV LAB, Knorrpromenade 9, 10245 Berlin, Germany**. Sign-in is provided through Parallel Vision's authentication service. Keep your account secure and use an email address at which you can receive service communications.

2. **Prices and ordering.** The checkout displays the selected product, currency and total before you confirm payment. Proposed catalog: €10 / 1,000 credits, €29 / 3,000 credits, €75 / 8,000 credits; monthly subscriptions €15 / 1,600 credits, €39 / 4,500 credits, €99 / 12,000 credits. **Confirm VAT treatment and final gross prices before making this clause effective.** Do not represent an unfinished tax setup as VAT-compliant.

3. **Credit delivery.** Credits are added only after the payment processor confirms a successful payment. If payment is pending, delayed or declined, returning to the website does not create a credit entitlement in the application ledger. Contact support if a completed payment does not appear. Duplicate delivery of the same payment notification does not add credits twice.

4. **Generating work.** The application presents the applicable generation credit charge or estimate before submission. Confirming generation reserves credits. A provider-confirmed failed generation returns its reserved credit charge once. If a request's status is uncertain, the reservation remains until reconciliation; automatically resubmitting may create a second charge. Request support using the job reference. Generation outputs can vary and are not guaranteed to reproduce a person, style or exact composition.

5. **Subscriptions.** The selected plan renews monthly until canceled according to the cancellation effective date shown in billing management. Credits are issued for verified paid billing cycles. Access billing management through the Credits panel. **Before enabling subscriptions, verify the cancellation configuration and state whether cancellation takes effect immediately or at period end.** Do not enable plan upgrades, prorations or discounts until their credit treatment is implemented and tested.

6. **Unused credits.** The current application does not apply automatic credit expiry. No expiry or forfeiture policy should be introduced through this draft. Monetary refund and withdrawal rights must be handled under the finalized terms and applicable law. Credit balances cannot currently be transferred between customer accounts through the application.

7. **Files and storage.** The current account upload allowance is 20 GiB; it is not increased by purchasing additional generation credits. Saved outputs remain in the private gallery until deleted or the Lab account is closed. Unused source uploads may be removed after 24 hours; sources referenced by jobs, saved packs or moods are protected. Download and independently back up work you need. The service is not offered as permanent backup storage.

8. **Rights and permitted inputs.** Upload only material you have the right to use, including the necessary permission for recognizable people and any required data-protection basis. Do not create or upload child sexual abuse material, non-consensual sexual imagery, unlawful threats, fraudulent impersonation or content that infringes others' rights. Generation also depends on the selected provider's applicable terms and restrictions. No third-party restriction is bypassed by these terms.

9. **Outputs.** These terms do not transfer ownership of your uploads to PV Lab. You authorize the processing needed to provide the requested service. Rights in generated output depend on applicable law, your inputs and the selected model/provider terms; the service does not promise copyright, exclusivity or freedom from third-party rights. No separate permission to publish your private images for advertising is implied by using the service.

10. **Refunds and withdrawal.** Contact **contact@parallelvisionlabel.com** with your receipt for payment errors, duplicate charges, refund requests or statutory withdrawal requests. Returned generation credits are separate from a refund to a payment method. Statutory consumer rights remain unaffected. **Insert the approved withdrawal information and form before public sales.** Do not infer waiver from sign-in, payment or a redirect, and do not enable an immediate-performance waiver without the required separate consent and durable record.

11. **Refund/dispute review.** A verified payment refund or dispute pauses new purchases and generation while support reconciles the payment, used credits and any associated rewards. Billing management remains available. Support must complete this review using the payment and immutable credit records; a disputed payment is not automatically an allegation of customer wrongdoing.

12. **Closing your Lab account.** Download your work, cancel active recurring billing and resolve running or uncertain generation jobs first. The explicit closure control closes creative access and queues deletion of Lab creative data. Cleanup checks Stripe again and retries temporary service failures. Closing PV Lab does not delete your shared Parallel Vision login or Nina account. Contact support separately about that identity, provider-held data, refunds or legally retained records.

13. **Service changes and responsibility.** Applicable mandatory consumer rights and liabilities are not excluded by these terms. Any proposed limitation of liability, jurisdiction clause or change to paid entitlements must be reviewed for the actual contracting business and customer territories before inclusion. Do not add an unrestricted right to delete paid work or confiscate balances.
