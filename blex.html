Profile / Nocturnal Signal

Blex moves through the Parallel Vision archive as a nocturnal signal. His music carries the raw pressure and emotional weight of international rock, reassembled through contemporary electronic structures, club movement and shadowed melodic tension.

Born from the friction between song memory and club architecture, his sound does not behave like nostalgia. Guitars, voices and dramatic traces are pulled apart, rebuilt as hypnotic grooves, synthetic pulse and cinematic pressure.

In Berlin 2063, Blex opens the harder edge of Parallel Vision: heavy silhouettes, distorted signals and tracks that feel both physical and haunted.
