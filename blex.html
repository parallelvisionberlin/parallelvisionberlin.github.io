Profile / Nocturnal Signal

Blex is an Argentine producer and DJ with a sound built for late rooms, emotional pressure and controlled disorder.

Inside Parallel Vision, he becomes a nocturnal signal: direct, elegant and slightly dangerous. His music carries the attitude of club culture without losing the feeling of a song, moving between physical rhythm, romantic distortion and the darker side of dance music.

Across Dark Rock, Ravesia and the Parallel Vision archive, Blex opens a sharper zone of Berlin 2063: red light, black leather, unstable desire and tracks that stay in the body after the room disappears.
