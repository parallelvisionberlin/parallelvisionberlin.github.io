Liquid Memory

Amatista(444) enters Fashion After Fabric as a body caught between water, crystal and signal.

Her image does not behave like a portrait. It feels like a transmission from inside the Parallel Vision archive: skin, fabric and atmosphere merging into one unstable surface. The garment becomes part of the weather around her, dissolving the line between person, material and environment.

In Berlin 2063, Amatista(444) represents the emotional side of future fashion: fragile, magnetic and impossible to fully separate from the world that surrounds her.
