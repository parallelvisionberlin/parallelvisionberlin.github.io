Press Release / Berlin 2063

Berlin has always been obsessed with the future.

For more than a century, the city has attracted people carrying their own visions of tomorrow. Tanzen Im Kreis was written from inside that contradiction: a place where abandoned futures continue to shape everyday life, and where every new beginning carries the remains of another.

The release opens Parallel Vision, an original fictional world set in Berlin 2063.
