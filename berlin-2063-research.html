Research & world notes

Berlin 2063 unfolds through four connected studies: urban form, the systems that connect the city, its human and synthetic inhabitants, and nightlife. The sequence moves from the scale of buildings to encounters between bodies.

An ongoing artistic exploration through images, film, sound and material studies.
