Profile / Dream State

Awake Dreaming

Awake Dreaming is a duo moving between club performance, intimate visual language and dream-state electronics. Their work enters Parallel Vision through a softer but charged frequency: bodies, water, night, memory and the blurred edge between performance and ritual.

Their images move between live booth documentation and suspended, cinematic scenes, creating an identity that feels awake inside a dream rather than outside the club.
