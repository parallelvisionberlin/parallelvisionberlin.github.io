Release links / Privacy

Music & measurement.

Parallel Vision operates these music release pages. For questions about your data, contact contact@parallelvisionlabel.com.

Page and button counts

We record page loads and clicks on music-platform buttons, grouped by release, day/hour, platform, broad traffic source, campaign and ad tags, approximate country/region/city, device type, browser and operating system. We also count first audio-preview plays per page load and grouped time before a platform click. Location is estimated from network metadata and can be inaccurate, particularly with VPNs. We do not request GPS access or retain exact coordinates or full browser user-agent strings. These are counts, not confirmed streams or unique listeners. We do not store names, email addresses, full referring URLs, advertising click IDs or raw IP addresses in this reporting database.

Random event receipts are retained for up to three days to prevent duplicate counts. A keyed, hourly-changing network digest is used to limit automated requests and is removed within approximately one day. Aggregate counts remain available to the account that manages that smart link. Campaign labels should never contain personal information.

Optional Meta advertising measurement

When a smart link has a configured Meta Pixel, it loads by default unless your browser has a saved choice to turn it off. It sends Meta page and release views and clicks on music-platform buttons, with the release title, artist, release identifier and selected music service. Meta may use cookies and other identifiers to match activity to its users and measure advertising. The campaign link may also contain advertising parameters used by Meta when advertising measurement is enabled. See Meta’s privacy policy.

Your advertising choice is stored in your browser under pv-music-meta-consent-v1. Use Tracking settings on a release page to turn advertising measurement off or on. Turning it off does not prevent you from listening and stops future Meta events from this page; it does not remove events already sent to Meta.

Music platforms and infrastructure

Music buttons take you to external services such as Spotify, SoundCloud or YouTube, which apply their own privacy policies. Audio previews and artwork may be hosted on Cloudflare or the media host chosen for that release. The website and API use GitHub Pages and Cloudflare infrastructure, which receive ordinary technical request information. No music-platform login is required by these landing pages.

Creator workspace

The private creator uses Clerk sign-in and verifies each account on the server. Accounts can access their own smart links, reports and tracking settings. Release records and counters are stored in Cloudflare D1; uploaded artwork and audio are stored in a dedicated public release-media namespace. No payment information or PV LAB credit data is part of release-page measurement.
