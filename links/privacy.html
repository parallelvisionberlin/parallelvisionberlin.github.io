Release links / Privacy

Music & measurement.

Parallel Vision operates these music release pages. For questions about your data, contact contact@parallelvisionlabel.com.

Page and button counts

We record page loads and clicks on music-platform buttons, grouped by release, day, platform, broad traffic source and campaign name. These are counts, not confirmed streams or unique listeners. We do not store names, email addresses, full referring URLs, advertising click IDs or raw IP addresses in this reporting database.

Random event receipts are retained for up to three days to prevent duplicate counts. A keyed, hourly-changing network digest is used to limit automated requests and is removed within approximately one day. Aggregate counts remain available to the label. Campaign labels should never contain personal information.

Optional Meta advertising measurement

Meta Pixel loads only after you select Allow Meta. It sends Meta page and release views and clicks on music-platform buttons, with the release title, artist, release identifier and selected music service. Meta may use cookies and other identifiers to match activity to its users and measure advertising. The campaign link may also contain advertising parameters used by Meta after consent. See Meta’s privacy policy.

Your advertising choice is stored in your browser under pv-music-meta-consent-v1. Use Privacy choices on a release page to change or withdraw your choice. Declining does not prevent you from listening. Withdrawing consent stops future Meta events from this page; it does not remove events already sent to Meta.

Music platforms and infrastructure

Music buttons take you to external services such as Spotify, SoundCloud or YouTube, which apply their own privacy policies. Audio previews and artwork may be hosted on Cloudflare or the media host chosen for that release. The website and API use GitHub Pages and Cloudflare infrastructure, which receive ordinary technical request information. No music-platform login is required by these landing pages.

Owner workspace

The private creator uses Clerk sign-in and verifies owner access on the server. Release records and counters are stored in Cloudflare D1; uploaded artwork and audio are stored in a dedicated public release-media namespace. No payment information or PV LAB credit data is part of release-page measurement.
