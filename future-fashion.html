Parallel Vision / Material Research

Material Research for Bodies in Transition

A speculative material research project exploring clothing beyond textiles through responsive matter, biotechnology, synthetic systems and new relationships between technology and the human body.

Across its collections, garments become synthetic skin, protective architecture, memory systems and interfaces between the body and its environment.

The project exists across images, moving works, artist collaborations and future physical experiments.

Material Test 01

Reactive matter replacing fabric.

Fashion After Fabric explores clothing as a living interface where responsive materials, synthetic biology and engineered systems replace traditional textiles and become part of the human body.
