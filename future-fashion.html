A material future by Parallel Vision

What happens when clothing no longer follows the body, but begins responding to it?

What happens when the boundary between body and garment begins to disappear?

When clothing can alter the body, does it still express who we are, or does it begin to determine who we become?

Fashion After Fabric investigates these questions through material research, developing garments that no longer behave as passive objects but as responsive systems capable of interacting with the body.

Each collection proposes a different material hypothesis, exploring how new relationships between body and matter could reshape the future of clothing.

Parallel Vision / Material Research

Material Research for Bodies in Transition

Fashion After Fabric explores what clothing becomes when textiles are replaced by responsive, biological and synthetic systems.

Each collection develops a distinct material hypothesis—from liquid metal and programmable surfaces to engineered tissue, magnetic structures and living membranes.

Together, they form an evolving archive of possible relationships between clothing, technology and the human body.

Artist Collaboration / Moving Image

Worn by the Inhabitants

Fashion After Fabric expands through collaborations with musicians, performers and visual artists.

Artists are reimagined as inhabitants of Berlin 2063, wearing evolving material systems inside films, projected runways and live audiovisual environments.

The same garment may transform between bodies, performances and realities.
