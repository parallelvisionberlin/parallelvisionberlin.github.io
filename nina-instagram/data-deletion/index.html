Delete your data

Last updated: 15 October 2026

You can request removal of personal information stored by the Nina FOK Instagram messaging integration, including stored direct-message text, associated message identifiers, contact records and generated draft replies.

Contact: contact@parallelvisionlabel.com

How to request deletion

  1. Email the address above with the subject “Nina FOK Instagram data deletion”.
  2. Include the Instagram username you used to send messages to @ninafok2000. Do not include your Instagram password or any login codes.
  3. We may ask you to verify control of the account through an appropriate confirmation step before acting on the request.
  4. After verification, we will delete the relevant records from our active Nina Instagram integration where deletion is applicable, and confirm when the request has been handled. Requests are handled without undue delay and normally within one month under GDPR, subject to lawful extensions where applicable.

What is covered

The request covers data that this particular integration controls. Meta independently controls Instagram account and message records; requests to delete Meta's own copies must be handled through Instagram or Meta. Cloudflare backups and security logs may be retained for their applicable periods, and records needed to comply with legal duties may be kept to the extent required.

Other requests

You may also request access or correction, or ask a question about processing, using the same email. Read the full Nina Instagram privacy notice.
