Privacy notice

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This notice covers the Nina FOK Instagram messaging integration, including messages received via the official Instagram API at @ninafok2000. It is separate from the PV LAB privacy notice and from other Nina website conversations.

1. Controller and contact

Alejandro Molinari, Parallel Vision
Knorrpromenade 9, 10245 Berlin, Germany
contact@parallelvisionlabel.com

2. What data is processed

When a person sends a message to the Nina FOK Instagram account, the integration can receive the Instagram-scoped sender identifier, message identifier, message text, timestamps and related message-event metadata made available by Meta. It may also store the processing status, relevant conversation history and an AI-generated draft reply. Cloudflare may process technical request and security information for operation of the service.

We do not ask for passwords. An Instagram account name is not proof of someone’s identity outside Instagram. The integration does not use access to the separate private-memory database of Nina's other experiences.

3. How and why data is used

Messages are processed to test and operate Nina's Instagram messaging experience, prepare contextually relevant responses, maintain continuity within the same Instagram-scoped conversation, detect duplicate deliveries and protect the service. During controlled testing, the integration may automatically send AI-generated replies to Instagram accounts explicitly authorized as testers. For other accounts, it currently stores drafts without sending AI replies. Before broader automated messaging is enabled, this notice and the user-facing transparency measures will be reviewed and updated as needed.

The relevant legal basis for operating and securing this voluntary messaging interaction is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and testing the requested experience under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Other legal obligations, where applicable, are handled under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests.

4. Automated processing and providers

Nina FOK is a digital character. Draft text is generated with AI using the conversation and a public character profile. Automated text may be inaccurate. Meta provides the Instagram messaging platform and API. Cloudflare operates the webhook, database, security logging and Workers AI inference used for draft generation.

Data may be processed internationally, including outside the European Economic Area, according to the providers' services and applicable transfer arrangements. For information about specific recipients and safeguards, contact us. See the Meta Privacy Policy and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

5. Retention

Instagram message data and related drafts are stored while needed to operate, test, secure or troubleshoot this messaging integration, or to handle a conversation. Records are reviewed for continued necessity and should be deleted when no longer needed. There is not currently a fixed automatic deletion interval in the integration. You may request deletion at any time, subject to applicable legal requirements. Service logs and backups can follow separate provider retention processes.

6. Your privacy rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your information, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact contact@parallelvisionlabel.com. We may need proportionate confirmation that the Instagram account belongs to you. Never send a password, login code or identity document unless a lawful and necessary secure method has been agreed.

You may also complain to a data-protection supervisory authority, including the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

7. Request deletion

For a clear explanation of how to request deletion of data stored by this integration, visit the Nina Instagram data-deletion page. Deleting a message inside Instagram does not necessarily remove a previously stored integration copy, and this integration cannot erase records held independently by Meta.

8. Updates

This notice will be updated if the integration or its data practices change materially. The revision date is shown above.
