Research & world notes

This alternate Berlin imagines the aftermath of World War III. Fear, masks and lonely streets shape its cyberpunk atmosphere. Rave offers a temporary place for bodies to gather within a fractured city. The research moves between damaged infrastructure, private refuges and the need for human contact after collapse.

One of three parallel possibilities in Berlin 2063 and Beyond, explored through images, film, sound and material studies.
