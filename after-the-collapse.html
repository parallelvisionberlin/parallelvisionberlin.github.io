After the Collapse

The city that remains

After the Third World War, Berlin survives in fragments. Its machines maintain the routines of a world that no longer exists.

Shelter

Life moves into old U-Bahn tunnels, gathering around water, medicine and whatever still functions. Above ground, masks and protective clothing mediate every encounter with the damaged city. Clean air and shelter determine who can stay, and who must keep moving.

Memory

Photographs, recordings and worn objects preserve what automated systems cannot understand. Some communities guard these traces; others fear that preserving the old world will also preserve its violence. Rebuilding begins with deciding what to carry forward.

After dark

Beneath the lonely streets, people still gather to dance. Clubs shelter unrecorded conversations and bodies exhausted by vigilance. Music, touch and private rituals sustain a shared life. Desire gives people a reason to seek one another beyond survival.
