WE BUILD WORLDS PEOPLE CAN ENTER.

Parallel Vision is a creative technology studio working across film, artificial intelligence, spatial media, interactive worlds and performance campaigns.
From an image to a film. From a flat film to a spatial experience. From a campaign to a world. Strategy, production and technology are developed as one system.
AI CINEMAIMMERSIVE FILMVR / SPATIALDIGITAL HUMANSINTERACTIVE WEBSTORYBOARDSINSTALLATIONSCAMPAIGNSSOUND

Not content for content's sake. We create visual systems, experiences and campaigns built to make an idea impossible to ignore.

Parallel Vision grew from music, moving image and worldbuilding. We combine creative direction with emerging production tools to create work that can move between screens, physical spaces and virtual environments without losing its identity.
