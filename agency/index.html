01 / What you can commission

Four ways to work with us.

Start with a single project, connect services around a launch, or build an ongoing creative partnership.

01

Films & visual campaigns

Campaign films, fashion editorials and music videos with a distinct visual identity, from the first storyboard to the final cut.

Art direction · AI-assisted production · Campaign imagery

Explore film deliverables

Concepts, visual research, storyboards, shot lists and animatics. Character development, Seedance and image-to-video production, editing, colour and finishing.

Original music, sound design and versions for social, advertising and web can be included in the brief.

02

Websites & digital characters

Launch websites, interactive portfolios and brand experiences. Digital characters can add a voice, a personality and a story.

Web design & build · Interactive storytelling · AI characters

Explore digital deliverables

Responsive design and development, film-led pages, galleries and virtual exhibitions. Character projects can combine visual identity, voice, knowledge and conversational memory.

Advanced 3D and WebXR features begin with a feasibility prototype. Hosting, integrations and ongoing support are agreed separately.

03

Immersive experiences

Take moving image beyond a flat screen through depth-based video, headset presentations and visuals for physical installations.

2D-to-3D studies · VR-ready content · Projection & display

Explore spatial deliverables

Footage assessment, a stereoscopic test and content prepared for the agreed headset or display. Projection and holographic-display content are developed around the space, with installation collaborators where needed.

Prototype first: hardware and format are confirmed before production. Depth conversion is not native 360° capture or a freely explorable 3D scene.

04

Creative & Meta Ads

Bring the idea, ad creative and distribution together. Plan the audience, test different approaches and refine the campaign.

Audience research · Ad creatives · Testing & retargeting

Explore campaign deliverables

Campaign strategy, audience research, creative variants, landing pages, account setup, retargeting and reporting. Pixel, conversion events and Conversions API work are scoped to the project.

Media spend is separate. Measurement and tracking permissions are checked before launch. Results are not guaranteed.

Also available on their own: storyboards, campaign imagery, original music and sound design.
