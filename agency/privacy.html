Enquiry privacy.

This notice covers the project brief form and basic activity measurement on the Parallel Vision Agency website.

Who receives your brief

Alejandro Molinari / Parallel Vision, Berlin. Contact: contact@parallelvisionlabel.com.

What we use

The form collects your name, email address, optional organisation, project direction, optional budget and project description. We also record the submission date, a receipt identifier and a broad source category such as Instagram or a direct visit. We use these details to assess and respond to your project enquiry. Submitting a brief does not subscribe you to marketing. Please do not include passwords, financial account details or sensitive personal information.

Storage and notifications

Briefs are stored in a private Cloudflare database accessible through an owner-authorised inbox. Resend is used to send a notification containing the brief to the studio's contact email. Briefs are kept in this enquiry database for up to 90 days, with daily deletion of older records. Email correspondence or records relating to an agreed commission may be retained separately as needed for that work.

Basic activity and abuse prevention

We count page views, form starts, contact clicks, film plays and accepted briefs as daily totals. These counts are not unique-person counts. This agency measurement does not use advertising cookies, store complete referring URLs or build visitor profiles. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals disable optional activity measurement. To protect the form, short-lived keyed digests derived from network addresses and submitted email addresses are used for rate limits. Raw network addresses are not stored in the agency tables. Expired rate-limit records are deleted by daily maintenance. Aggregate activity records are retained for 90 days.

Films and external links

The embedded YouTube player is loaded only after you choose to play that film. Playback connects to YouTube and is subject to its privacy practices. Other project links may take you to separate websites.

Questions or removal

Write to contact@parallelvisionlabel.com to ask about, correct or request removal of your enquiry. Include your receipt reference where available.
